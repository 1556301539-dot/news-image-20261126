Every story on the front page happened, or was first reported, on

## The three big stories

1. **HORMUZ — Five more tankers hit mines (CLAIM).**
   Iranian armed forces claimed at least five more oil tankers hit sea
   mines and caught fire in the past 24 hours after using
   "unauthorised routes" (Times Now live blog, 26 Nov, 09:28 IST).
   UKMTO reported an explosion from an unknown projectile near a
   tanker about 50 nautical miles west of Dubai on Thursday
   (UKMTO via Reuters, 26 Nov). Brent crude $94.71 a barrel at
   01:37 GMT (Reuters, 26 Nov). Earlier claims (two tankers, one LNG
   carrier) were made on Wednesday 25 Nov and are NOT used as today's
   news. Sources: Times Now, Reuters, UKMTO.
2. **THANKSGIVING STORM — Macy's parade postponed, first time in
   100 years (OFFICIAL).** New York postponed the 100th Macy's
   Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday 28 November over a major
   nor'easter, a first in the parade's 100-year history (AFP, dateline
   New York, 26 Nov; Malay Mail, 26 Nov 10:05 MYT). National Weather
   Service high wind warning from 10 a.m. 26 Nov, gusts 60-65 mph;
   coastal flood warning. Mayor Zohran Mamdani briefed the city on
   Wednesday. The parade, running since 1924, was last missed in
   1944 (WWII suspension). Note: the postponement was announced on
   25 Nov; the storm, the warnings and Thanksgiving Day itself are
   26 Nov. Sources: AFP, Malay Mail, USA Today, NBC.
3. **NORTH KOREA — Kim vows to expand nuclear weapons (REPORTED).**
   Kim Jong Un inspected nuclear materials production facilities and
   called for wider deployment of tactical nuclear weapons and more
   nuclear weapons, after ignoring Trump's overture (AP, 26 Nov;
   Economic Times, 26 Nov 10:15 IST; Reuters summary, 26 Nov).
   Sources: AP, Reuters, Economic Times.
